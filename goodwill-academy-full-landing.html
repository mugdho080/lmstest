Frequently Asked Questions

Is Level 1 really free?

Yes! Our Level 1 modules are completely free. Just sign up to start learning.

How do I unlock Level 2 with my NDIS plan?

Click “Unlock with NDIS Plan” and fill out the form. We'll contact your plan manager or support coordinator to get approval. No charges without your consent.

What about Level 3?

Level 3 involves community-based experiences and 1:1 support. Our team will help you design a personalised learning path.

What data do you collect?

We only collect the information needed to create your account and track your learning. All data is stored securely and handled under strict privacy standards.
