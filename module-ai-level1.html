Lesson 1 – What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) means computers that can learn patterns and make smart guesses. They do not “think” like humans, but they can help with tasks.

  • AI can recognise pictures and voices (for example, your phone’s voice assistant).
  • AI can suggest videos, songs or shows based on what you watched before.
  • AI can help people with disability by providing captions, reading text out loud, or translating languages.

Key idea: AI is a tool. People are still in charge. You can choose when to use it and when to say no.
