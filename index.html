How it works

Same theme everywhere, so learners always know what to expect.

Step-by-step flow

  1. Open any chapter and begin with the free Level 1 lesson.
  2. Sign up (name, email, NDIS, plan manager) to unlock every Level 2+ instantly.
  3. Admins can review clients on the Admin page and lock/unlock access.
  4. Add new chapters or levels by editing assets/js/chapters.js.
🧠
Tip: Keep sentences short and buttons large. All spacing and colors come from assets/css/theme.css so pages stay consistent.

Design choices for autistic learners

  • ✅ Soft gradients and generous white space.
  • ✅ Reduced motion and clear focus states.
  • ✅ Short sentences with icons to anchor attention.
  • ✅ Calm progress blocks instead of flashing badges.

Platform features

Everything runs on simple HTML, CSS, and JS—easy to host on GoDaddy.
